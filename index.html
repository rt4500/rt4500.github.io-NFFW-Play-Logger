Logging

Both shows an OFFENSE / DEFENSE switch on the log screen. On defense you log the opponent's formation/play and your own defensive call.

Team

Off = down/distance hidden while logging and left blank in the export. You can still set the down on any single play with the “–/1/2/3/4” buttons.
On = a “ball is here” bar under the situation card. It moves itself by whatever each play gained, so most of the time you never touch it. Feeds the yard line in the Hudl export and the new “By field position” stats.
Swiss 5-on-5 is usually a 50-yard field with drives from your own 5. Change these if your league plays differently.
One tap per snap the other team takes. It adds a placeholder row to the export so it lines up with the film clip for clip — otherwise a Hudl import has to guess which clips are yours. The markers never appear in your stats. Only shown when you're logging offence only.
Save turns orange once if you logged yardage but never tapped a result — tap it again to save anyway. Those half-logged plays are invisible in completion %, passer rating and receiver stats.
Distance to gain comes from where the ball is: midfield first, then the end zone once you cross it. Needs field position on. Off = the old behaviour, counting down from the default distance.
A–Z keeps every play call in the same spot all game. Most used puts the calls you run often at the front. Starred favourites stay on top either way.

Display & feel

Sunlight = larger text and heavier borders. Direct sun = black on white with no greys, no shadows and bigger buttons, for a tablet with the sun on it. The 🔅 button in the header cycles through them without leaving the Log tab.

Roster

Everyone can be picked as passer (trick plays). Tap QB to mark your usual passers — they show first while logging. ✕ removes.

Claude

The assistant coach on the Stats tab works out its findings on this device with no network. Open in Claude hands the same briefing to the Claude app for a deeper read — that uses your normal subscription.

Playbook & value lists

Hudl export

Import in Hudl: open the game video → Data → Import Data → upload this CSV. Column names match your existing Hudl breakdown (DN, DIST, PLAY TYPE, RESULT, GN/LS, ODK, …).

Merge with film breakdown

Already tagged the defence in Hudl? Export that breakdown, drop it in here, and you get one file with both sides — your offence on the offensive clips, your defensive tagging untouched. Pick a single game above first.

Backup & recovery

Restore replaces everything on this device with what's in the file. To pull in one game without losing what's already here, use the next button instead.
Two people logging on two tablets ends up as two half-seasons. Point this at the other device's backup file, or at a Hudl CSV this app exported, and pick the games to bring across — everything already on this device stays put.
Taken automatically at the end of every game and every 25 plays, in a separate store from the app's main copy. Use one if something goes wrong — a bad restore, a game deleted by mistake, or storage cleared out from under you.

Team file (private roster & playbook)

Encrypts your current roster & playbook (AES-256) into team.enc. Host that file next to your index.html. Each device taps “Sync team,” enters the password once, and loads the presets — the names & play calls are never in the public app. Change your roster/playbook later? Re-download team.enc, replace it, and everyone re-syncs. Pick a strong passphrase — the encrypted file is public.
